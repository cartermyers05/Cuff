# Retail Investor Adoption of LLMs/AI Chatbots for Stock Research and Trade Ideas (as of Oct 2026)

Scope note: These notes size how many retail investors and traders ask ChatGPT, Claude and similar tools for research and plays, how much they trust and act on the answers, what platforms disclose, what the outcome evidence shows, and what regulators have said. Every survey below defines "AI" differently. The notes say for each one whether it means chatbots specifically, any AI tool, or a broad "use or open to using" measure. Treat cross-survey comparisons as directional only.

## 1. Adoption: what share of investors use AI chatbots for investing research or stock picks (US first, then global; trend 2023 to 2026; demographics; use cases)

### Takeaway
By 2026, between about one-third and three-fifths of US investors say they have used AI (mostly ChatGPT-style chatbots) for investing or financial information. Only about 10–15% of investors globally say they currently use chatbots to actually pick or change stocks. The figures run highest among Gen Z and millennials, and ChatGPT is the dominant tool, used by roughly two-thirds to three-quarters of AI-using investors.

### Cited Findings

**US surveys (most recent first)**
- **Investing.com survey, fielded Mar 3–10, 2026. n=938 US adult investors; how respondents were recruited is not disclosed.**
  - 62% have used AI tools to inform investment decisions: 23.6% regularly, 27.4% occasionally, 11.5% once or twice. Another 21.0% have not used AI but are considering it; 16.6% do not plan to.
  - Among those who have experimented with AI, 53.5% use AI chatbots such as ChatGPT for investing research. Other tools: AI research or stock-screening tools 30.6%, AI portfolio management tools 6.4%, automated trading algorithms 4.5%.
  - Use cases: researching stocks or other assets 62.4%, understanding market news 35%, **generating trading ideas 34.4%**, helping make portfolio decisions 21.7%. The base for these figures is not stated.
  - Caveat: Investing.com sells its own AI product (WarrenAI), which is a potential conflict of interest.
  - Source: [Investing.com blog](https://www.investing.com/blog/how-retail-investors-are-using-ai-in-2026-339); also [Investing.com news](https://www.investing.com/news/stock-market-news/survey-nearly-twothirds-of-retail-investors-use-ai-to-inform-market-decisions-4598846).
- **Betterment 2026 Retail Investor Survey, fielded late March 2026. n=1,000 US investors.**
  - 48% of Gen Z investors say AI has already influenced a financial decision they made.
  - 41% of Gen Z are comfortable using AI for long-term planning.
  - Baby boomers: 5% on both measures.
  - Source: [Fortune, Sept 18, 2026](https://fortune.com/2026/09/18/gen-z-ai-investing-advisors-financial-planning-sports-betting/).
- **Betterment Advisor Solutions survey, fielded June 2026. n=1,001 people with an advisor.**
  - 65% of Gen Z clients say AI influenced a decision they otherwise would not have made.
  - For comparison, 24% of retail investors with no advisor said the same.
  - Source: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/18/gen-z-ai-investing-advisors-financial-planning-sports-betting/).
- **Vanguard Investment Strategy Group survey. n=6,686 Vanguard personal investors, fielded "June".**
  - The article does not state the year. It appears to be 2026, based on the article's ID sitting next to a Sept 2026 InvestmentNews story; this is not confirmed.
  - About 1 in 3 investors overall have used AI for help with personal finance, investing or retirement.
  - By generation: Gen Z and millennials 43%, Gen X 41%, boomers and older 15%.
  - "AI" here means AI for financial guidance, mostly conversational AI.
  - Source: [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/fintech/ai-financial-advice-lifts-demand-for-human-advisors-says-vanguard/268433).
- **J.D. Power consumer surveys (all consumers, not investors only; personal finance, not investing specifically).**
  - Sept 2025: 51% said they turn to AI for financial advice or information. Source: [ABA Banking Journal](https://bankingjournal.aba.com/2025/09/survey-consumers-increasingly-turn-to-ai-for-financial-advice/).
  - June 2026, n=4,000: 40% have sought help from AI to manage their personal finances, and 27% found it somewhat or significantly helpful. Source: [Finopotamus](https://www.finopotamus.com/post/jd-power-report-finds-ai-in-tight-competition-with-traditional-financial-advising). This is a secondary write-up and was not checked against the J.D. Power release.
- **Academic survey: Blankespoor et al. (University of Washington), working paper presented 2025. n=2,175 retail investors.**
  - Nearly half are already using generative AI, mainly to gather and interpret financial information.
  - Among GenAI users: ChatGPT 74.6%, Google Gemini 37.3%, Microsoft Copilot 35.8%.
  - Source: [Generative AI and Investor Processing of Financial Information (PDF)](https://www.herbert.miami.edu/_assets/pdfs/faculty-research/business-conferences/winter-warmup/2025/paper-5_blankespoor.pdf).
  - A Sept 2026 academic paper summarizes the survey literature as "over 40% of retail investors report using GenAI tools to inform their investment decisions," citing eToro 2023, Oliver Wyman 2024 and Blankespoor et al. 2026. Source: [He, "When ChatGPT Stops Talking" (AFA PDF)](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=257180).
- **Motley Fool, fielded Sept 6, 2024 via Pollfish. n=2,000 US adults who already use generative AI to help make investment decisions.**
  - Because every respondent was already an AI user, this survey cannot estimate the adoption rate. It is useful for tool mix and use cases.
  - Tools used: ChatGPT 66%, Google Gemini 42%, Microsoft Copilot 37%, Perplexity 17%, **Anthropic Claude 15%**, other 3%.
  - Use cases: stock analysis and research 57%, market trend analysis 51%, summarizing financial news 38%, **making the final buy/sell decision 35%**, summarizing SEC filings and managing portfolios about 25% each.
  - Using AI for every investment decision: Gen Z about 20%, millennials 26%, boomers 9%.
  - Using AI for the final buy/sell decision: Gen X 41%, Gen Z and millennials about 35%, boomers 25%.
  - Investors with 3–5 years of experience use AI most often.
  - Source: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/research/survey-how-investors-are-using-generative-ai).
- **Motley Fool, April 2023 via Pollfish. n=2,000.**
  - 47% of ChatGPT users said they had asked it for stock recommendations.
  - The Fool itself said the figure "may be high" and likely reflects the hype at the time.
  - Sources: [Business Today](https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/over-47-percent-of-us-adults-use-chatgpt-for-stock-advice-survey-shows-382097-2023-05-20); [Wealth Professional](https://www.wealthprofessional.ca/news/industry-news/47-of-americans-rely-on-chatgpt-for-stock-picks-study/375834); [Motley Fool, May 2023](https://www.fool.com/investing/2023/05/08/survey-women-skeptical-chatgpt-stock-picks/).
- **eToro 2023, US cut. n=1,000 US respondents.**
  - 41% of US retail investors are open to, or already using, AI tools such as ChatGPT to pick or alter their portfolios. This combines current users with people who are only open to it.
  - 34% believe AI will pick better investments than a fund manager.
  - Source: [eToro US press release](https://www.etoro.com/en-us/news-and-analysis/latest-news/press-release/four-in-ten-u-s-retail-investors-use-or-are-open-to-using-chat-gpt-style-ai-to-help-pick-and-manage-investments/).
- **eToro, late 2025, US. n=1,000. Secondary source only.**
  - 58% use AI tools to help build their portfolios, and 30% use AI to pick or alter investments.
  - This comes from a Substack summary and was not verified against an eToro release. Source: [Mindful Data by Cindy (Substack)](https://mindfuldatabycindy.substack.com/p/ai-and-the-rise-of-the-diy-retail).
- **FINRA Foundation National Financial Capability Study 2024 Investor Survey (published late 2025). n=2,861 US investors with non-retirement accounts.**
  - The report contains **no question on AI or chatbots**. A text search of the PDF found no mentions of AI, ChatGPT or generative AI.
  - Related channel data:
    - 26% of investors use recommendations from social media finfluencers, including 61% of investors under 35.
    - YouTube is used by 30% of investors for investment information.
    - 13% have bought meme stocks or other viral investments, including 29% of investors under 35.
  - Source: [FINRA Foundation NFCS Investor report (PDF)](https://www.finrafoundation.org/sites/finrafoundation/files/2025-11/NFCS_Investor_Survey_Report_White_Paper.pdf).
- **FINRA, Dec 11, 2025 report on social media-influenced investing.**
  - Citing the FINRA Foundation's 2025 study, it says 45% of investors receive financial advice from the internet and 24% get information from social media.
  - By age: 35% of those under 30 rely on social media, versus 13% of those 65 and older.
  - Source: [FINRA report](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/key-topics/fintech/report/social-media-influenced-investing).

**Global surveys**
- **eToro Retail Investor Beat, Aug 13–28, 2026, by Opinium. n=11,000 retail investors in 13 countries; respondents did not have to be eToro users.**
  - 56% "already use or are open to using AI tools, such as ChatGPT or AI agents, to help pick or alter investments."
  - This is a combined use-or-open measure; current users are not broken out.
  - Source: [eToro press release, Oct 8, 2026](https://www.etoro.com/news-and-analysis/press-releases/your-ai-already-runs-your-day-now-it-knows-your-portfolio/).
- **eToro Retail Investor Beat 2025. n=11,000 retail investors worldwide.**
  - 13% already use AI tools such as ChatGPT or Gemini to pick stocks.
  - About half would consider using these tools to pick or alter portfolio investments.
  - Reuters' lede: "at least one in 10 retail investors" uses a chatbot to pick stocks.
  - Reuters noted that OpenAI has not released data on how many people use ChatGPT to choose investments.
  - Source: [Reuters via The Star, Sept 25, 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/09/25/analysis-039chatgpt-what-stocks-should-i-buy039-ai-fuels-boom-in-robo-advisory-market--).
- **eToro Retail Investor Beat, June 2–14, 2023, by Opinium. n=10,000 retail investors in 13 countries.**
  - 11% already use ChatGPT-style AI to help pick or alter investments, 35% are open to it, and about 40% are opposed.
  - Finance Magnates reported the opposed share as 41%, so the reports differ slightly.
  - Usage among 34–44-year-olds was 19%.
  - Sources: [eToro press release](https://www.etoro.com/news-and-analysis/press-releases/one-in-ten-retail-investors-using-chat-gpt-style-ai-to-help-pick-and-manage-investments/); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/41-of-10000-retail-investors-firmly-reject-chatgpt-for-investment/).
- **HSBC "Trust Threshold," Jan 6 – Feb 6, 2026, by Ipsos, released June 24, 2026. n=9,993 affluent and high-net-worth investors aged 21–69 in 10 markets including the US and UK.**
  - 73% use AI for finance and investment, the top area of their AI use. That rises to 82% among high-net-worth investors, 86% among Gen Z and 82% among millennials.
  - Uses: analysis and research 66%, strategy support 50%, second opinion on ideas 31%.
  - "AI" here means any AI use for finance and investment, among affluent investors only.
  - Source: [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/the-trust-threshold-ai-makes-investors-bolder-but-they-want-human-judgement-to-make-decisions).

  - Roughly one-third of young investors have used generative AI for financial education.
  - Source: [CFA Institute press release](https://www.cfainstitute.org/about/press-room/2026/next-gen-investors-report-march-2026).
- **Finder, UK.**
  - 40% of respondents have used chatbots or AI for personal finance advice.
  - Source: [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/09/25/analysis-039chatgpt-what-stocks-should-i-buy039-ai-fuels-boom-in-robo-advisory-market--).

### Inferences
- **Trend from 2023 to 2026**
  - On eToro's like-for-like global measure, the share who "currently use chatbots to pick or alter investments" crept from 11% (2023) to 13% (2025).
  - Broader measures, such as having used AI to inform decisions or for financial guidance, reach 33–62% in 2026 US surveys.
  - The gap is mostly definitional. Many investors use chatbots for research and explanation; far fewer let them choose the stock.
  - The use-or-open share rose from 41% (US, 2023) and 46% (global, 2023: 11% + 35%) to 56% (global, 2026). That is a modest rise in openness, not a step change.
- **Working US estimate for sizing**
  - Roughly 1 in 3 to 1 in 2 US investors have used an AI chatbot for investing research.
  - Roughly 1 in 3 of those, about 10–20% of all investors, have used one to generate trade ideas or make a final buy/sell decision.
  - The figures are higher among under-45 and active traders. Investing.com's 62% and 34.4% likely skew high because its respondents are an engaged investing-site audience; this is an assumption, since sampling was not disclosed.
- **Tool share**
  - ChatGPT dominates, with about 66–75% of AI-using investors.
  - Gemini and Copilot each have about 35–42%.
  - Perplexity (17%) and Claude (15%) were secondary as of 2024.
  - A connector strategy should prioritize ChatGPT first. Claude and Perplexity users are fewer but plausibly more power-user heavy; this is an inference, not measured.
- **Demographics**
  - Usage is consistently highest among Gen Z and millennials, at roughly 40–86% depending on the measure.
  - It is lowest among boomers, at roughly 5–15%.
  - Under-35 investors also lean heavily on finfluencers (61% per FINRA), a group that may be receptive to "sentiment plus track-record" data.

### Gaps
- No found 2025–2026 data on AI or chatbot investing use from Broadridge, Public.com, Bankrate, Investopedia, MarketWatch Guides, or Schwab's investor-side surveys. The Schwab figures found cover RIAs, not retail investors: 63% of RIAs use AI tools, per a third-party aggregator, unverified.
- The 2024 FINRA Foundation investor survey did not ask about AI at all, so there is no FINRA-grade, nationally representative estimate of chatbot use for investing.
- The eToro 2026 release does not split current users from people who are only open to it.
- The late-2025 eToro US figures (58% and 30%) are secondary only.
- Search snippets cited a TD Bank survey and a "VISTA 2026" survey, possibly SIFMA/KPMG. Neither was fetched:
  - TD Bank: 55% of Gen Z and millennials used AI to help make financial decisions, up from 10%; 18% would trust AI to independently make recommendations.
  - VISTA 2026: Gen Z trust in AI-only advice fell to 10% from 20%; n=2,100+.
  - The publisher and methodology could not be verified. Reported by [FinanceFeeds](https://financefeeds.com/vista-survey-finds-investors-still-favors-human-financial-advice/) and [Financial Advisor magazine](https://www.fa-mag.com/news/gen-z-trust-in-ai-only-advice-plunges-by-half-as-faith-in-advisors-surges-88873.html).
- No survey isolates "active traders" (frequent traders, options traders) from long-term investors on chatbot use.
- No total US retail investor or trader population base was researched here, which is needed to convert percentages into headcounts.

## 2. Trust and action: how much investors trust AI output, and how often they act on it

### Takeaway
Most investors say they trust AI investing output only "somewhat" and verify it elsewhere. Human advisers remain more trusted by a wide margin. Even so, roughly a third of AI-using US investors say they have acted on an AI trade idea, while very few, about 4% of younger Vanguard investors, let AI make decisions for them.

### Cited Findings
- **Investing.com, Mar 2026 (n=938 US).**
  - Trust in AI-generated analysis: completely 3.8%, mostly 19.8%, somewhat but verify elsewhere 53.5%, not much 12.7%, not at all 10.2%.
  - **Acting on AI trade ideas: followed multiple times 26.6%, followed once 8.9%, not yet but may consider 20.9%, never 43.7%.**
  - Expected future use: much more 37.8%, slightly more 17.3%.
  - Top concerns: incorrect or misleading recommendations 38.9%, **market herding from shared signals 24.2%**, over-reliance on automation 21.0%.
  - Source: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/blog/how-retail-investors-are-using-ai-in-2026-339).
- **Vanguard (n=6,686).**
  - Gen Z, millennial and Gen X investors: 63% report little or no trust in conversational AI guidance; 5–6% report high trust.
  - Boomers: 78% little or no trust; 2% high trust.
  - Human advisers: 54–63% of every generation highly trust adviser guidance.
  - **Only 4% of younger investors use AI to make decisions on their behalf.** 80–85% in every age group are uncomfortable with AI acting for them.
  - Investors are about 3x more likely to use AI to prepare for or review an adviser meeting than to replace one.
  - About 4 in 10 said chatbots beat human advisers at avoiding conflicts of interest.
  - Source: [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/fintech/ai-financial-advice-lifts-demand-for-human-advisors-says-vanguard/268433).
- **HSBC, 2026 (affluent, 10 markets).**
  - 32% say their last investment idea came from AI; 62% say it came from professionals and institutions.
  - **12% say AI was the most influential factor in their last investment decision.**
  - 50% prefer a hybrid of AI and adviser.
  - 49% say AI makes them more willing to take calculated risks, versus 20% who say it makes them more cautious.
  - **In the US, 44% say AI makes them more willing to take calculated risks.** By generation: Gen Z 59%, millennials 58%.
  - Source: [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/the-trust-threshold-ai-makes-investors-bolder-but-they-want-human-judgement-to-make-decisions).
- **Motley Fool, Sept 2024 (n=2,000 AI-using investors).**
  - 35% use AI for the final buy/sell decision.
  - Only 14% think AI is less accurate than traditional sources. No group reached 50% saying it is more accurate; about half call it equally accurate.
  - Source: [Motley Fool](https://www.fool.com/research/survey-how-investors-are-using-generative-ai).
- **Northwestern Mutual 2025 Planning & Progress Study (Harris Poll, n=4,626 US adults).**
  - 56% would trust humans more than AI to build a retirement plan; 13% would trust AI more.
  - 47% would prefer an adviser who understands and uses AI.
  - Source: [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/human-connection-over-machines-americans-trust-advisors-more-than-ai-for-financial-advice-finds-northwestern-mutuals-2025-planning--progress-study-302520924.html).
- **Betterment, 2026.**
  - 48% of Gen Z investors say AI influenced a financial decision.
  - 65% of advised Gen Z clients say AI influenced a decision they otherwise would not have made.
  - Source: [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/18/gen-z-ai-investing-advisors-financial-planning-sports-betting/).
- **CFA Institute, Mar 2026.**
  - Human advisers are "the single-most-trusted source of investment guidance" for next-gen investors.
  - Source: [CFA Institute](https://www.cfainstitute.org/about/press-room/2026/next-gen-investors-report-march-2026).

### Inferences
- The "verify elsewhere" habit (53.5% in the Investing.com survey) and the accuracy worries point to demand for better grounding data:
  - Vanguard: 37% of AI users received incorrect information, and 44% could not tell whether it was wrong.
  - Investing.com: 38.9% worry about incorrect recommendations.
  - This supports a business that feeds verified sentiment, track records and news into the user's LLM.
- "Acted on an AI idea at least once" (about 35% of the Investing.com sample) and "AI decides for me" (4%, Vanguard) bracket the action rate.
- The relevant action for a data vendor is the first one: AI informs a trade the human then executes.

### Gaps
- No survey measures how much money or how many trades follow AI suggestions, or the holding period or outcome of AI-sourced trades.
- No found trust data specific to Claude or Perplexity versus ChatGPT.

## 3. Platform usage evidence (OpenAI, Anthropic, Perplexity, broker AI features, ChatGPT Finances, connectors)

### Takeaway
No AI lab has published the share of chatbot conversations about investing. The usage signals are indirect: eToro and OpenAI have launched in-chat brokerage and finance connections, Robinhood's Cortex has reached about 1 million customers, and about 100,000 Robinhood users have opened agentic trading accounts.

### Cited Findings
- **OpenAI "How People Use ChatGPT" (Sept 2025).**
  - It samples about 1.1 million conversations from May 15, 2024 to June 26, 2025.
  - Practical Guidance, Seeking Information and Writing make up about 80% of conversations.
  - By intent, messages split into Asking about 49%, Doing about 40% and Expressing about 11%.
  - A full-text search of the paper found **no finance- or investing-specific topic share** (no hits for "financ" or "stock").
  - Source: [OpenAI paper PDF](https://cdn.openai.com/pdf/a253471f-8260-40c6-a2cc-aa93fe9f142e/economic-research-chatgpt-usage-paper.pdf).
  - Reuters (Sept 2025): OpenAI "has not released data on how many people use ChatGPT to choose investments." Source: [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/09/25/analysis-039chatgpt-what-stocks-should-i-buy039-ai-fuels-boom-in-robo-advisory-market--).
- **Academic proxy: Paydarzarnaghi & Karami (SSRN, 2026), "What Do People Ask AI About Finance? Evidence from ChatGPT."**
  - Analyzes 7,548 finance-related conversations drawn from about 1 million real interactions in the public WildChat corpus, a third-party ChatGPT front-end, not OpenAI's own logs.
  - Source: [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=6999350); summarized via search, full text blocked (403).
- **ChatGPT Finances.**
  - Announced May 15, 2026 as a preview for US Pro users, extended to Plus in June 2026.
  - **Rolled out to US Free and Go users on Oct 2, 2026**, on web, iOS and Android, in stages.
  - Bank and brokerage accounts connect through Plaid; credit reports connect through Experian.
  - Dashboards cover spending, net worth and investment portfolios.
  - It is read-only: no trades, transfers or bill payments.
  - Sources: [OpenAI release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes); [OpenAI personal finance page](https://openai.com/index/personal-finance-chatgpt/); [Eigen Radar, Oct 3, 2026](https://eigenradar.com/en/ai/briefs/2026-10-03/chatgpt-finances-free-go-access/); [DataStudios](https://www.datastudios.org/post/openai-finances-chatgpt-free-go-connected-accounts-spending-savings-investments).
  - No adoption figures were found.
- **eToro AI connectors, launched Oct 8, 2026.**
  - Covers Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Grok Bot, built on the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**.
  - No API keys or code needed. Access is read-only for research, or "read-and-act" where available, with user approval required for proposed actions.
  - **Not available to clients in the US, Australia or Singapore.**
  - No usage figures were disclosed.
  - Source: [eToro press release](https://www.etoro.com/news-and-analysis/press-releases/your-ai-already-runs-your-day-now-it-knows-your-portfolio/).
- **Robinhood.**
  - Q1 2026 results (Apr 28, 2026): Cortex Digests have been "used by nearly 1 million customers to date," and Cortex Assistant is rolling out to all Gold customers. Sources: [Robinhood Q1 2026 results](https://investors.robinhood.com/news-releases/news-release-details/robinhood-reports-first-quarter-2026-results); [Q1 2026 call transcript](https://investors.robinhood.com/static-files/c2119020-41a1-4008-b9ae-db1b9fd6fb5e).
  - Q2 2026 call (July 29, 2026): Robinhood launched v1 of agentic trading, which lets customers build AI agents to trade equities, options and crypto. The CEO said **over 100,000 people integrated and opened agentic accounts**. Friction noted: setup requires a code editor, and models can be "hesitant to trade." Sources: [Q2 2026 call transcript](https://investors.robinhood.com/static-files/f44ea493-9c52-4744-9671-7720e1ac7b2f); [Yahoo Finance highlights](https://ca.finance.yahoo.com/news/robinhood-markets-inc-hood-q2-050029710.html). The Q2 details are from search summaries of these pages.
- **Investing.com WarrenAI (platform data).**
  - About 2.79 million WarrenAI interactions from Sept 1, 2025 to Aug 31, 2026.
  - The US ranked 19th of 66 countries on Investing.com's AI Investing Index; Italy led.
  - Top US states per 1,000 users: Florida about 451, Texas about 329, California about 321.
  - Source: [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/fintech/ai-investing-takes-hold-far-beyond-wall-street-new-data-shows/268478).
- **Perplexity.**
  - The CEO disclosed 780 million monthly queries in May 2025, up from about 230 million in Aug 2024.
  - No finance-specific share is published.
  - User counts come only from third-party estimates.
  - Sources: [TSG Invest](https://tsginvest.com/perplexity-ai/); [Quash](https://quashbugs.com/blog/perplexity-ai-statistics-revenue-users-queries-and-valuation). These are secondary SEO statistics sites; treat as estimates.
- **Anthropic Economic Index.**
  - Recent reports (Jan and Mar 2026) emphasize coding (about one-third of Claude.ai traffic is computer and math tasks) and rising personal use (35% to 42%).
  - No finance or investing share was found in the summaries reviewed.
  - Sources: [Medium summary](https://medium.com/@ZombieCodeKill/claude-reviews-anthropics-economic-index-ed44052c6e12); [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/experienced-claude-users-succeed-10-more-and-the-gap-is-widening/).

### Inferences
- **WildChat proxy**
  - 7,548 of about 1 million conversations is roughly 0.75% finance-related in that corpus.
  - This is my calculation from the paper's reported counts. The paper's filter and WildChat's user base, which skews toward developers and non-US users, make it a weak proxy for ChatGPT's real share.
- **Rough scale**
  - Even if investing were well under 1% of ChatGPT traffic, ChatGPT's scale would still mean a very large absolute number of investing queries.
  - No first-party number lets this be sized precisely.
- **Market direction**
  - eToro's MCP connectors and OpenAI's Plaid-linked Finances both show distribution moving toward "bring your portfolio and data into the chatbot," the same channel the user's business would sell into.
  - eToro's exclusion of US clients suggests US regulatory or operational caution about in-chat brokerage. This is speculation: the release gives no reason.

### Gaps
- No usage disclosures were found for:
  - Public.com Alpha
  - Google Finance AI
  - Perplexity Finance specifically
  - Similarweb or Sensor Tower traffic to finance-AI apps
  - Google Trends data for "ChatGPT stock picks"
- No adoption numbers for ChatGPT Finances or eToro connectors, since both launched within the last three weeks.

## 4. Behavior and outcomes: do AI-assisted retail investors trade differently or perform better or worse; herding evidence

### Takeaway
Early causal evidence, mostly working papers that use ChatGPT outages and Italy's 2023 ChatGPT ban as natural experiments, shows that ChatGPT broadens what retail investors trade but also makes their order flow more correlated, a form of herding. There is no evidence that this correlated component predicts returns. Performance evidence is self-reported or anecdotal.

### Cited Findings
- **He (Lancaster), "When ChatGPT Stops Talking: GenAI, Retail Trading Coordination, and Market Liquidity" (Sept 2026 version).**
  - Uses 14 unexpected ChatGPT outages as natural experiments.
  - When ChatGPT is available, **retail order flow is more correlated**, and more information-related flow moves together across stocks.
  - This common component "shows little evidence of return predictability" and appears to substitute for the informative individual component.
  - ChatGPT makes retail selling less informative and retail buying more informative.
  - It also raises **liquidity commonality**, meaning greater exposure to market-wide liquidity shocks.
  - The paper notes that regulators (ESMA 2023, BIS 2024, FSB 2024, IMF 2024, Bank of England 2025) have flagged "GenAI-induced herding."
  - Source: [AFA PDF](https://afajof.org/management/viewp.php?n=257180).
  - A related version describes ChatGPT as a "belief homogenizer" that reflects collective overreaction. Source: [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/403105680_When_ChatGPT_Stops_Talking_GenAI-induced_Retail_Herding_and_Systematic_Risk); summarized via search.
- **Even-Tov, Lourie, Munevar & Nekrasov, "The Effect of AI on Retail Investor Behavior: Evidence from Account-Level Trading Data" (SSRN).**
  - Uses Italy's temporary ChatGPT ban.
  - During the ban, investors concentrated trades in fewer assets, were less likely to open new positions, and shifted toward popular assets.
  - The authors conclude GenAI broadens retail trading by lowering information-processing costs.
  - Source: [SSRN](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/Delivery.cfm/5778246.pdf?abstractid=5778246&mirid=1); summarized via search, full text not read.
- **Washington University in St. Louis (Olin), Apr 2025.**
  - Retail traders' trading aligned with ChatGPT sentiment almost immediately after ChatGPT's release, and "ChatGPT can level the playing field for retail investors."
  - Source: [WashU Olin news](https://olin.washu.edu/about/news-and-media/news/2025/04/chatgpt-level-playing-field-retail-investors.php).
- **Bertomeu, Lin, Liu & Ni, Journal of Accounting and Economics, 2025 (Italy ban).**
  - Domestic analysts issued fewer and less accurate forecasts during the ban, and bid-ask spreads widened.
  - This shows that losing GenAI access reduced market efficiency, though it concerns analysts, not retail investors.
  - Source: [IDEAS/RePEc](https://ideas.repec.org/a/eee/jaecon/v80y2025i1s0165410125000187.html).
- **LLM forecast bias.**
  - Research shows LLM stock-return forecasts display extrapolation and miscalibration, similar to human biases.
  - Source: [arXiv 2409.11540](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2409.11540).
- **Self-reported performance**
  - Investing.com 2026: among AI users, 17.1% say performance improved significantly and 47.6% somewhat (about 65% combined); 33.3% see no difference; 1.9% say it got worse. Source: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/blog/how-retail-investors-are-using-ai-in-2026-339).
  - HSBC 2026: 90% estimate AI influenced part of their returns, attributing 33% of returns to AI on average (39% for investors with $2M+). Source: [HSBC](https://www.hsbc.com/news-and-views/news/media-releases/2026/the-trust-threshold-ai-makes-investors-bolder-but-they-want-human-judgement-to-make-decisions).
- **Accuracy experienced by users.**
  - 37% of Vanguard AI users received incorrect or misleading information, and 44% could not tell.
  - Source: [InvestmentNews](https://www.investmentnews.com/fintech/ai-financial-advice-lifts-demand-for-human-advisors-says-vanguard/268433).
- **Anecdotal test: Finder.**
  - A 38-stock ChatGPT-picked basket from Mar 2023 rose about 55% by Sept 2025, about 19 points above the average of the UK's 10 most popular funds.
  - This is not a controlled study.
  - Source: [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/09/25/analysis-039chatgpt-what-stocks-should-i-buy039-ai-fuels-boom-in-robo-advisory-market--).
- **Investor sentiment about herding.**
  - 24.2% of the Investing.com sample cite herding from shared AI signals as a concern.
  - Source: [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/blog/how-retail-investors-are-using-ai-in-2026-339).

### Inferences
- The herding and homogenization finding is directly relevant to the user's pitch: if everyone's LLM draws on the same generic web data, signals converge.
- Differentiated, scored data such as trader track records and human sentiment could be positioned as a counter to homogenized outputs. This is a positioning inference, not tested.
- Self-reported performance gains (about 65%) are unreliable and likely reflect hindsight bias in a bull market. No study with measured returns shows that AI-assisted retail investors outperform.

### Gaps
- No broker has published account-level returns for AI-feature users versus non-users.
- No study was found on herding into specific "ChatGPT-recommended" tickers, such as abnormal volume in stocks LLMs commonly recommend.
- Most evidence is from working papers and may change.

## 5. Regulatory: SEC, FINRA and state warnings or rules on AI investment advice, AI finfluencers, AI-washing; relevance to a data-to-LLM vendor

### Takeaway
US regulators have not created AI-specific rules for retail chatbot advice. They rely on existing antifraud, supervision, recordkeeping and communications rules, and have brought AI-washing enforcement cases. Retail-facing warnings have focused on AI-themed fraud and deepfakes, and FINRA opened a consultation on social media-influenced investing in Dec 2025.

### Cited Findings
- **Joint investor alert, Jan 2024.**
  - The SEC, NASAA and FINRA issued "Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Investment Fraud."
  - It warns that bad actors exploit AI hype, including deepfake audio and video.
  - Sources: [Investor.gov](https://www.investor.gov/introduction-investing/general-resources/news-alerts/alerts-bulletins/investor-alerts/artificial-intelligence-fraud); [FINRA](https://www.finra.org/investors/insights/artificial-intelligence-and-investment-fraud).
- **FINRA 2025 Annual Regulatory Oversight Report.**
  - Firms using GenAI for chatbot communications with investors must supervise them and retain chat sessions under SEC and FINRA rules.
  - FINRA has seen increased GenAI use by bad actors.
  - Sources: [FINRA 2025 report (PDF)](https://www.finra.org/sites/default/files/2025-01/2025-annual-regulatory-oversight-report.pdf); [Debevoise summary](https://www.debevoisedatablog.com/2025/02/05/finras-2025-regulatory-oversight-report-on-artificial-intelligence/).
- **FINRA Regulatory Notice 24-09.**
  - Reminds firms that existing rules apply when they use GenAI.
  - Source: [FINRA AI key topics page](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/key-topics/artificial-intelligence).
- **Secondary: Bretton Woods Committee brief, Apr 2026.**
  - The SEC withdrew its proposed predictive data analytics / AI conflicts rules for advisers and broker-dealers in June 2025.
  - FINRA's 2026 report names Rule 3110 (supervision) in its GenAI section.
  - Source: [Bretton Woods Committee brief (PDF)](https://brettonwoods.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/BWC-AI-FinancialAdvice-brief-A-1.pdf). Secondary; verify against SEC and FINRA originals.
- **FINRA report on social media-influenced investing (Dec 11, 2025).**
  - Raises awareness among member firms.
  - Notes that "market participants are increasingly seeking to harness social media data for market analyses and product development."
  - FINRA engaged more than two dozen parties, including software service providers.
  - Requests comment on all areas, with comments due May 13, 2026.
  - The fetched introduction did not mention AI.
  - Sources: [FINRA](https://www.finra.org/rules-guidance/key-topics/fintech/report/social-media-influenced-investing); [Free Writings law blog](https://www.freewritings.law/2025/12/finra-publishes-report-on-social-media%E2%80%91influenced-investing/).
- **SEC AI-washing enforcement (secondary summaries).**
  - Mar 2024: Delphia ($225,000) and Global Predictions ($175,000), the first AI-washing cases against advisers, charged alongside Marketing Rule violations.
  - Jan 2025: Presto Automation, the first public-company AI-washing case.
  - Apr 2025: the SEC and DOJ charged Nate founder Albert Saniger, alleging the promised automation was largely done by humans.
  - A separate case: an adviser falsely described as running an "AI-driven platform for automated trading" raised nearly $4M from 45 investors.
  - The SEC created its Cyber and Emerging Technologies Unit (CETU) in Feb 2025, and enforcement has continued under Chair Atkins.
  - Sources: [Sidley FY2025 review](https://www.sidley.com/en/insights/newsupdates/2025/10/2025-fiscal-year-in-review-sec-enforcement-against-investment-advisers); [StoneTurn](https://stoneturn.com/insight/next-generation-compliance-preparing-for-continued-sec-ai-washing-enforcement/); [Seward & Kissel 40 Act Blog](https://40actblog.sewkis.com/insights/sec-charges-investment-company-ceo-and-board-member-for-alleged-misleading-statements-regarding-use-of-artificial-intelligence/).
- **Chatbots and fiduciary duty.**
  - A consumer-finance article says general chatbots carry no fiduciary duty and are not subject to SEC oversight for the advice they give, and quotes a FINRA alert saying AI content "may appear authoritative but may lack the rigor of professionally vetted financial analysis."
  - The quote could not be traced to an original FINRA document. Source: [Stockpil](https://stockpil.com/ai-financial-advice-caution) (low reliability).
- **Industry warnings.**
  - eToro's UK managing director: "The risk comes when people treat generic models like ChatGPT or Gemini as crystal balls"; they "can misquote figures and dates."
  - Source: [Reuters via The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2025/09/25/analysis-039chatgpt-what-stocks-should-i-buy039-ai-fuels-boom-in-robo-advisory-market--).

### Inferences
- **For a vendor feeding data into users' LLMs via MCP**
  - The most relevant exposures appear to be AI-washing and antifraud risk:
    - claims about the accuracy or predictive power of "scored trader track records" or sentiment
    - Marketing Rule-style performance claims, if any registered adviser or broker relationship exists
  - FINRA's interest in firms harnessing social media data and finfluencer content is also relevant, if the product scores social media traders.
  - If the vendor partners with brokers, as eToro did, broker-dealer supervision and recordkeeping duties (FINRA Rule 3110; chat retention) flow to the broker's use of the data.
- Track-record scoring of identifiable social media "traders" may draw scrutiny under FINRA's finfluencer work if brokers use it in communications. This is an inference; no specific rule was found.

### Gaps
- No SEC or FINRA rule or guidance was found that specifically addresses third-party data vendors supplying data to consumers' general-purpose LLMs.
- Whether such a vendor could be deemed an "investment adviser," or would rely on the publisher's exclusion (Lowe v. SEC, 1985, from background knowledge, not researched here), needs legal review.
- No 2026 SEC or FINRA investor alert specifically about asking chatbots for stock picks was found. The newest official joint AI alert found is from Jan 2024.
- No state-level (NASAA or individual state) AI-chatbot advice rules were found in this pass.
- No non-US regulatory detail was gathered (ESMA, FCA) beyond the He paper's citation of ESMA 2023, BIS 2024, FSB 2024, IMF 2024 and BoE 2025 herding warnings.
