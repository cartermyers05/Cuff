# Brokerages and Trading Apps With Social/Community Features Competing With StockTwits

Notes current as of Oct 21, 2026. US focus, with international notes where they matter (eToro, Futu/Moomoo, Trading 212). Older items carry dates. Several 2026 figures come from secondary trade press (Finance Magnates, Yahoo Finance editorial, Investing.com transcript summaries) rather than primary filings, and are flagged where relevant.

## Robinhood: Robinhood Social (verified trades, follows) and Cortex/AI features

### Takeaway
Robinhood Social is real and fully launched, which makes it the most direct threat to a "calls on the record" stock-chat app. It was announced Sept 9-10, 2025 (HOOD Summit, Las Vegas), entered an invite-only beta on Mar 18, 2026 (about 1,000 users, then 10,000 more), and opened to all eligible US customers between late Sept and mid-Oct 2026. Every profile is KYC-verified, posted trades are verified live, and profiles show P&L and profit rate. The verification is opt-in and selective, though: users choose which trades to post and can hide their stats. The feature is also locked to Robinhood customers. No engagement numbers have been disclosed.

### Cited Findings
**Announcement (Sept 2025)**
- At HOOD Summit 2025, Robinhood announced Robinhood Social as "a new trading community within the Robinhood app featuring live, verified trades and authentic profiles." Users can see where traders enter and exit positions, discuss strategies, follow traders, and start a trade directly from the feed. Profiles show 1-year and daily P&L, profit rate, and past trades. Each profile is verified through KYC. Robinhood billed it as the first social platform for trading stocks, options, futures, crypto and prediction markets without switching apps. It is free, with an invite-only launch planned for early 2026. — [Robinhood Newsroom, HOOD Summit 2025 (Sept 10, 2025)](https://robinhood.com/newsroom/hood-summit-2025-news/)
- The 2025 announcement also included following "insiders, hedge funds, and politicians based on publicly reported trades." Robinhood said this data comes from third-party provider TipRanks, that Robinhood does not verify it, and that these people are not Robinhood customers. — [Robinhood Newsroom, HOOD Summit 2025](https://robinhood.com/newsroom/hood-summit-2025-news/)
- Press framing: Axios headlined it "Robinhood takes on Reddit and X with new social media platform." Its search snippet notes that verification is built in because users can only post their own trades. — [Axios (Sept 10, 2025)](https://www.axios.com/2025/09/10/reddit-x-robinhood-social-meme-stock). Zacks called it a copy-trading platform in which users copy trades manually. — [Zacks](https://www.zacks.com/stock/news/2749686/hood-unveils-copy-trading-platform-robinhood-social-at-annual-summit)

**Beta (Mar 2026)**
- On Mar 18, 2026 the beta began rolling out to "a select group of our most active and engaged traders." It started with 1,000 customers who attended HOOD Summit in September, with 10,000 more to follow "over the coming weeks." It was invitation-only, with selection based on trading activity and frequency plus UX research. Beta features: profiles with stats and live-verified trades, posting, following, comments, likes, and trading from the feed. Posts could cover stocks, single-leg options, crypto and prediction markets. Planned for later in the beta: news feeds, futures and multi-leg posting, and following insiders, hedge funds and politicians. The release said moderation policies would be "refined during beta." The legal entity is Robinhood Tape, LLC. — [Robinhood Newsroom, "Robinhood Social is Now in Beta" (Mar 18, 2026)](https://robinhood.com/newsroom/robinhood-social-beta/)
- Android Headlines also reported the initial beta group as 1,000 highly active users. — [Android Headlines (Mar 2026)](https://www.androidheadlines.com/2026/03/robinhood-verified-social-trading-network-beta.html)

**General availability (Sept-Oct 2026)**
- HOOD Summit '26 was held Sept 29, 2026 in Houston. Coverage says Social is out of beta and officially launched, with verified trading signals, real P&L data, portfolio allocations and position posts. It pitches the product as a fix for fake trades, wash trading and "Telegram-style signal scams." It also describes curated feeds of market commentators, companies, politicians, CEOs and news, plus a Social widget for Legend (desktop) showing trade cards with entry and exit points. The coverage gives no engagement numbers. — [Investing.com summary of HOOD Summit '26 transcript](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/robinhood-at-hood-summit-26-pushes-trading-into-living-hours-93CH-4923894)
- Robinhood's X account said on Sept 29, 2026 that Social had reached all customers. Vlad Tenev posted on Oct 13, 2026 that "Robinhood Social is now live for everyone" and that "When we started with verified trades, we knew it was just the first step." Legend gets Social "in the coming weeks." The user agreement, effective Sept 18, 2026, states Social "is NOT a copy trading platform" and is not for professional investment advice. It bars securities-licensed professionals, people associated with brokerages or exchanges, and Robinhood staff. — [Finance Magnates via CoinSpectator (Oct 14, 2026)](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2026/10/14/robinhood-lets-all-eligible-us-customers-share-their-trades-and-portfolios/)
- Rollout to all eligible US customers was dated Tuesday, Oct 13, 2026. Users can share charts, P&L and portfolio allocations, follow traders, and use "Quick trade" to place an eligible trade directly from a post. **Customers choose which eligible transactions to share and can turn off profile trading statistics.** Futures and multi-leg options cannot be posted. The product is US-only. Robinhood also flagged a "live earnings experience" for Q3 earnings season, where customers can watch earnings calls and trade in real time. The article gives no post or user totals. — [Yahoo Finance (Oct 13, 2026)](https://finance.yahoo.com/markets/stocks/articles/robinhood-expands-social-trading-push-000800407.html)
- Robinhood's Social landing page says it shows "the moves made by Robinhood traders, politicians, industry insiders, influencers, and hedge funds." It disclaims that customer posts "do not represent the views or recommendations of Robinhood." — [Robinhood Social page](https://www.robinhood.com/us/en/social/)

**Scale and strategy**
- Robinhood had 28.4M funded customers at the end of June 2026. — [Finance Magnates via CoinSpectator](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2026/10/14/robinhood-lets-all-eligible-us-customers-share-their-trades-and-portfolios/)
- On the Q2 2026 earnings call, Tenev framed Social as a way to help customers learn from one another, because Robinhood has mostly been a place to execute trades whose ideas came from elsewhere. Transcript quality varies across providers, so exact wording should be checked against the audio. — [Motley Fool Q2 2026 transcript](https://www.fool.com/earnings/call-transcripts/2026/08/07/robinhood-hood-q2-2026-earnings-call-transcript/)
- More than 15 products were announced at HOOD Summit '26, including AI trading agents and crypto perpetual futures. — [Finance Magnates via CoinSpectator](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2026/10/14/robinhood-lets-all-eligible-us-customers-share-their-trades-and-portfolios/)

**Cortex and other AI features**
- Cortex is Robinhood's AI investing assistant, available only to Robinhood Gold members ($5/month). — [Robinhood Support: Cortex](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cortex)
- Cortex Digests (AI summaries of why a stock is moving) launched in the US in summer 2025 and in the UK in Aug 2025. — [IBS Intelligence](https://ibsintelligence.com/ibsi-news/robinhood-launches-ai-powered-digests-in-uk); [Robinhood Support: Cortex Digests](https://robinhood.com/us/en/support/articles/cortex-digests)
- At HOOD Summit 2025, Robinhood announced Cortex-built custom indicators and custom scans, where users describe what they want in natural language. Both were slated for Gold customers in early 2026. — [Robinhood Newsroom, HOOD Summit 2025](https://robinhood.com/newsroom/hood-summit-2025-news/)
- At HOOD Summit '26, Robinhood presented "Robinhood Agents," AI trading agents in a separate agentic account with trade approvals on by default. It also showed "Agent Apps" from third-party data providers, including Unusual Whales, Nasdaq and QuiverQuant. Robinhood claimed more than 150,000 agentic accounts opened since the May 2026 launch and agents using Robinhood tools more than 30M times a day. — [Investing.com HOOD Summit '26 summary](https://investing.com/news/transcripts/robinhood-at-hood-summit-26-pushes-trading-into-living-hours-93CH-4923894). These are company claims from a transcript summary, not audited figures.

### Inferences
- This is the closest competitor to "calls on the record." Robinhood offers a KYC-verified identity, live-verified entries and exits, and a public P&L and profit-rate profile. It also has distribution to about 28M funded customers, which a pre-launch app cannot match.
- The verification has structural holes that a standalone app can exploit:
  - **Selective disclosure.** Users pick which trades to post and can hide stats, so a profile can still be cherry-picked. It is not a complete, unbiased record.
  - **Broker lock-in.** Only Robinhood customers can produce verified posts. The many retail traders at Schwab, Fidelity, IBKR or Webull are excluded.
  - **Excluded voices.** Licensed professionals and people tied to brokers or exchanges are barred.
  - **Unsupported trades.** Futures and multi-leg options cannot be posted.
  - **Position-based, not forecast-based.** A trade proves money moved. It does not capture a directional view on a stock the user doesn't hold, such as a bearish view held without shorting.
  - **Trade-driving incentive.** Quick trade from posts and the live-earnings trading feature show the feed is built to generate order flow. A neutral, no-execution venue can position itself against that.
- Robinhood's own framing ("ideas came from elsewhere") concedes that ideas currently come from Reddit, X and StockTwits-type venues. Social is an explicit attempt to pull that idea flow into the app.

### Gaps
- No Robinhood Social engagement metrics (DAU, posts, follows, share of customers posting) appeared in any source found. The Q2 2026 call and HOOD Summit '26 coverage give none.
- No systematic user reception was found, such as app-store review analysis, Reddit sentiment or tech-press hands-on reviews after GA. One search returned only unrelated 2021 GameStop-era coverage.
- Whether the politician, insider and hedge-fund follows actually shipped at GA is unconfirmed. The 2025 announcement promised them, HOOD Summit '26 coverage mentions politician feeds, and the beta release listed them as "coming later."
- Exact GA date: the Sept 29 X post and the Oct 13 Tenev post/Yahoo article give two dates. A staged rollout between them is likely.

## eToro: social feed, CopyTrader, Popular Investor program, public-company metrics

### Takeaway
eToro is the original "social investing" broker. Social features drive its business through CopyTrader and a Popular Investor program that pays copied traders up to 1.5% of assets under copy per year. It IPO'd on Nasdaq (ETOR) on May 14, 2025 at $52 and had 4.28M funded accounts at end of Q2 2026. Its US footprint is still limited: CopyTrader is in a phased US rollout, and it is buying TradeZero to expand in the US. That makes it a smaller near-term threat to a US stock-chat app than Robinhood.

### Cited Findings
- **IPO:** eToro priced its upsized IPO at $52/share (11,923,018 Class A shares, above the initial $46-50 range). Trading began on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on May 14, 2025 under "ETOR." — [eToro pricing release, BusinessWire (May 13, 2025)](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20250513117690/en/eToro-Announces-Pricing-of-Upsized-Initial-Public-Offering). It opened at $69.69 and closed its first day at $67.00. — [The Block](https://www.theblock.co/post/354207/etoro-prices-nasdaq-debut-52-surpassing-expectations)
- **Q2 2026 (reported Aug 11, 2026):**
  - Funded accounts were 4.28M, up 18% YoY from 3.63M, and 4.32M in July.
  - Net contribution was $229M, up 9% YoY.
  - eToro agreed to acquire TradeZero, a US online brokerage for active traders.
  - AUA was reported as $19.2B or over $19B at quarter end and $18.5B in July (down 5% YoY). Outlets differ on these figures.
  - Sources: [eToro press release](https://www.etoro.com/news-and-analysis/press-releases/etoro-reports-second-quarter-2026-results/); [eToro 6-K via StockTitan](https://www.stocktitan.net/sec-filings/ETOR/6-k-e-toro-group-ltd-current-report-foreign-issuer-37e4bfe21bf6.html)
- **Stock reaction to Q2 2026:** reports conflict. Investing.com says shares fell 8.2% — [Investing.com](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-etoro-posts-q2-2026-profit-beat-as-shares-fall-82-93CH-4851898). Another outlet cited -13.91% for the Aug 11 session.
- **FY2025 (reported Feb 17, 2026):** net contribution was $868M (+10%) and GAAP net income $216M (+12%). The release gives inconsistent AUA figures ($18.5B, +11%, versus $18.4B, +2%). — [eToro FY2025 results](https://investors.etoro.com/news-releases/news-release-details/etoro-reports-fourth-quarter-and-full-year-2025-results)
- **Registered users:** third-party pages variously cite "over 40m" and "over 30m." The company figure was not confirmed. — [Capital.com eToro IPO explainer](https://capital.com/en-int/learn/ipo/etoro-ipo)
- **Popular Investor program:**
  - It is built on CopyTrader and lets investors "build up their business by sharing their expertise and expanding their assets under copy (AUC)."
  - It pays up to 1.5% of AUC per year, paid monthly, at Champion tier and above.
  - Tiers require minimum copier counts and minimum average AUC.
  - Sources: [eToro Popular Investor page](https://www.etoro.com/copytrader/popular-investor/); [eToro PI tiers](https://etoro.com/popular-investor)
- **Copy eligibility tightened:** from June 15, 2025, only Popular Investors can be copied. — [FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/etoro-updates-copytrader-to-permit-copying-of-popular-investors-only/)
- **US status:**
  - eToro's US CopyTrader page says the feature is available "to a select group of US users as part of a phased roll out," and the US Popular Investor program is "stay tuned." — [eToro US CopyTrader](https://go.etoro.com/en/USCopyTrader)
  - US users can currently copy only US-based Popular Investors. — [Finder](https://www.finder.com/investments/etoro-copy-trading)
  - Finance Magnates reports eToro brought automated CopyTrader mirroring to US investors in Oct 2025. — [Finance Magnates via CoinSpectator](https://coinspectator.com/mainstream/2026/10/14/robinhood-lets-all-eligible-us-customers-share-their-trades-and-portfolios/)
  - A much earlier US crypto-only CopyTrader launch dates to Oct 2019. — [Bitcoinist](https://bitcoinist.com/etoro-launches-copytrader-for-u-s-users/)
- **Positioning:** eToro describes itself as a social network with the ability to trade and invest. Its flagship features are copy trading and user-generated portfolios. — [Capital.com](https://capital.com/en-int/learn/ipo/etoro-ipo)
- **Industry context:** Brokeree data shows copy trading made up 6-20% of brokerages' trading volume in recent years. — [Finance Magnates (June 2025)](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/publiccom-terminates-social-trading/)
- **Twitter/X link:** X first tested cashtag-to-broker links with eToro alone in 2023. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/webull-joins-x-cashtag-program-as-social-feed-adds-broker-links/)

### Inferences
- eToro proves social features can be monetized at scale, but its model is copying (AUC-based payouts), not open stock chat. Its "verified track record" is the copied portfolio's performance, which is broker-locked and limited to Popular Investors since June 2025.
- In the US, eToro's social product is still in a phased rollout and its US stock brokerage presence is small. The pending TradeZero deal signals intent to grow in US equities, so it is a medium-term watch item rather than an immediate threat.

### Gaps
- No disclosed count of Popular Investors, total assets under copy, total PI payouts, or share of funded accounts that copy, from FY2025 or Q2 2026 materials found. The F-1, 10-K and 20-F were not reviewed.
- No US-specific eToro user or funded-account numbers were found.
- TradeZero deal terms and closing status were not researched.

## Public.com: original social features, removal, current AI (Alpha) positioning

### Takeaway
Public.com, once marketed as "the social investing app," shut down its social feed and social trading features on June 5, 2025. It argued that AI now produces the earnings recaps and market updates users used to post. It now positions itself as a multi-asset brokerage led by AI tools: Alpha (launched May 2023) and Generated Assets (launched May 2025). It is no longer a social competitor, and its exit is a useful data point about broker-run feeds.

### Cited Findings
- Public announced on Thursday, June 5, 2025 that it was ending its social trading services, which let users see other traders' activity and market insights. In the blog post "Sunsetting Social," it wrote: "The core use cases of the social feed used to be people posting recaps of earnings calls" and "Now, this exact content is created by AI—faster, more accurate, and embedded throughout the product experience." Public had marketed itself as "the social investing app." It now positions as a multi-asset platform (stocks, options, bonds, margin, and crypto via Bakkt). — [Finance Magnates (June 6, 2025)](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/publiccom-terminates-social-trading/); [FX News Group](https://fxnewsgroup.com/forex-news/retail-forex/public-shuts-down-social-trading/)
- Generated Assets, an AI feature that turns a thematic idea into a custom equity index, launched in May 2025. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/publiccom-terminates-social-trading/)
- Alpha, Public's AI investing assistant built on OpenAI's GPT-4, launched in May 2023. Public has run brand campaigns around it. — [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/393886); [LBB Online](https://www.lbbonline.com/news/investing-platform-public-helps-retail-investors-with-ai-powered-investing-tool-alpha)
- The founders reportedly said Alpha was used by more than 90% of members by Dec 2024. This is a company-reported claim surfaced via search summary, and the exact attribution was not verified. — [Contrary Research report on Public](https://research.contrary.com/report/public)

### Inferences
- Public's exit suggests two things. Generic commentary content (recaps, news summaries) is being commoditized by AI. And a broker-run feed that doesn't drive trading or retention can be cut. A standalone app's value therefore needs to be something AI can't generate: accountable, timestamped, scored human predictions and the identity and reputation built on them.
- It also shows that broker social features are vulnerable to the broker's own priorities, which supports a neutral-venue pitch.

### Gaps
- No pre-shutdown engagement or user numbers for Public's feed were found.
- Current (2026) Alpha usage and capabilities, and Public's 2026 funded-account count, were not found.

## Webull and Moomoo (Futu): community size, features, engagement claims

### Takeaway
Both have large in-app communities tied to sizable user bases. Webull had 28.2M registered users and 5.13M funded accounts at end of June 2026. Futu (Moomoo/Futubull) had 31.25M registered users and 3.84M funded accounts at Q2 2026, mostly Asia-weighted. Neither discloses community engagement numbers. Both joined X's new Cashtag Partner Program in Sept-Oct 2026, which suggests brokers increasingly treat X, not their own feeds, as the main venue for stock discussion. Neither appears to offer Robinhood-style verified trade posting at scale (not confirmed).

### Cited Findings
**Webull**
- At end of June 2026, Webull had 28.2M registered users, 5.13M funded accounts and $28.5B in customer assets. — [Finance Magnates (Oct 2026)](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/webull-joins-x-cashtag-program-as-social-feed-adds-broker-links/)
- Webull runs an in-app community where members exchange ideas, and users must attest to Webull Community Guidelines. — [Webull AU Community page](https://webull.com.au/community)
- Since Jan 2024, US Webull users have had TradingView charts, analytics and community tools inside the app. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/webull-integrates-tradingviews-advanced-tools-for-traders-in-the-usa/)
- Corporate Connect lets public companies run investor-relations presences on Webull. For example, Upexi joined in June 2025 to reach "Webull's 24 million registered users." — [Finviz/press release](https://finviz.com/news/76361/upexi-joins-webull-corporate-connect-service-platform)
- Webull offers AI connectors for ChatGPT, Claude and Grok, plus a CLI and MCP, giving natural-language access to account data, research and orders. Launch date not confirmed. — [Webull newsroom](https://www.webull.com/news/15344695056475136)
- **X Cashtag Partner Program:** Webull joined on Oct 2, 2026. Tapping a cashtag on X shows a live chart, related posts and links to partner brokers, but X does not execute trades. Webull may use X sentiment and cashtag data in its products. Commercial terms were not disclosed. — [Webull newsroom](https://www.webull.com/news/15678771247793152); [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/webull-joins-x-cashtag-program-as-social-feed-adds-broker-links/)

**Futu / Moomoo**
- Q2 2026 (released ~Aug 20, 2026):

  - Brokerage accounts were 6.64M (+27%) and funded accounts 3.84M.
  - Client assets were about US$178.4B (+43.6%).
  - Revenue was US$918.2M (+35.6%).
  - No community DAU or post counts were given, and no US/international split.
  - Source: [Futu Q2 2026 results, posted on moomoo Community](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/futu-announces-q2-2026-results-strong-results-across-global-markets-117126789726214)
- Q1 2026: 30.17M registered users and 6.28M brokerage accounts. — [Futu Q1 2026 results on moomoo Community](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/futu-announces-q1-2026-results-robust-global-growth-sustained-with-116651215552518)
- Futu's Q3 2025 release said community daily active users "surged" and user-generated content accelerated, without giving numbers. — [PR Newswire (Q3 2025)](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/moomoos-parent-company-futu-releases-q3-2025-results-net-income-up-137-yoy-to-us425-7-million-302618326.html)
- Futu ended 2025 with 3.37M funded accounts and guided to 800K net new funded accounts in 2026. — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/futu-is-chasing-800000-new-clients-in-2026-after-a-record-year/)
- Moomoo was a launch partner in X's Cashtag Partner Program (Sept 15, 2026). — [Finance Magnates](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/webull-joins-x-cashtag-program-as-social-feed-adds-broker-links/)
- The moomoo Community also works as a content and IR distribution surface. Futu's own Q2 2026 results post drew about 68K views, and third-party earnings-call transcripts (e.g., Robinhood Q2 2026) are posted there. — [moomoo Community post](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/futu-announces-q2-2026-results-strong-results-across-global-markets-117126789726214); [moomoo Community, Robinhood Q2 2026 call](https://www.moomoo.com/community/feed/robinhood-q2-2026-earnings-conference-call-117005270122541)

### Inferences
- Webull and Moomoo communities are sizable but broker-locked and mostly unverified. They look more like an in-app StockTwits clone than a verified track-record product, so they threaten "calls on the record" less directly than Robinhood Social does.
- Webull, Moomoo, IBKR and Coinbase all partnering with X suggests that most brokers are choosing to buy distribution from the dominant open conversation venue rather than build their own social network. That makes X itself a large competitor in stock chat, with cashtag charts and broker links.

### Gaps
- No community-specific DAU, MAU or post-volume figures for Webull or Moomoo were found. Futu gives only qualitative claims.
- No US-only user counts for Moomoo were found.
- Whether Webull or Moomoo display verified positions or P&L on community profiles was not confirmed.

## Others: Schwab/thinkorswim, IBKR, Trading 212, Coinbase/Base, X Cashtags, Polymarket/Kalshi; Fidelity, SoFi, Cash App

### Takeaway
None of the other large US brokers has a verified-trade social network comparable to Robinhood's. Schwab has thinkorswim chat rooms (confirmed as of 2023). IBKR's social play is distribution via X Cashtags. Trading 212 (UK/EU) has in-app Communities. Coinbase's Base App (July 2025) is an onchain social feed rather than stock chat. Prediction markets compete as sentiment venues with money at stake, but their volume is dominated by sports. No social features were found for Fidelity, SoFi or Cash App within the research budget.

### Cited Findings
- **Schwab/thinkorswim:** Schwab's Oct 2023 launch of thinkorswim for Schwab clients described "the thinkorswim community," which "allows clients to share ideas and insights with other passionate traders via in-platform chat rooms." No newer source confirming current status was found. — [Schwab press release (Oct 2023)](https://pressroom.aboutschwab.com/press-releases/press-release/2023/Schwab-Introduces-Schwab-Trading-Powered-by-Ameritrade-Setting-a-New-Standard-for-the-Retail-Trading-Experience/default.aspx)

  - Launch partners were Interactive Brokers, Moomoo, Coinbase, Kraken and Gemini, and Webull joined Oct 2, 2026.
  - Searching or tapping a cashtag on X shows a live chart, posts about the asset and partner broker links, while trades are completed at the broker.
  - X tested this model with eToro alone in 2023.
  - Source: [Finance Magnates (Oct 2026)](https://www.financemagnates.com/forex/brokers/webull-joins-x-cashtag-program-as-social-feed-adds-broker-links/)
- **Interactive Brokers:** an X Cashtag launch partner (above). No IBKR-native social or community product was researched.
- **Trading 212 (UK/EU):**

## Do brokers' verified-trade social features undermine a standalone "calls on the record" app? What can a standalone app do that broker-locked communities can't?

### Takeaway
Partly. Robinhood Social now delivers KYC-verified identities, live-verified trades and public P&L and profit rate to roughly 28M funded customers, which weakens a "track record" pitch based only on self-reported calls. But broker verification is opt-in and selective, limited to one broker's customers, built to drive order flow, and tied to positions rather than forecasts. A neutral, cross-broker app that scores every call by the same rules (price at post, score at a fixed horizon, no deleting or cherry-picking) and is open to non-customers still has room to differentiate. The edge depends on enforcing completeness and a standard method, and it has to compete for distribution with Robinhood and X.

### Inferences
**Where brokers undermine the standalone pitch**
- Money on the line beats cheap talk. A verified, funded position is a stronger credibility signal than a free call, and Robinhood now displays it natively.
- Distribution: about 28M Robinhood funded customers, plus X's audience and cashtag charts, versus a pre-launch app.
- A combined "follow, see verified entry, tap to trade" loop is convenient, and a standalone app can't execute trades.

**What a standalone app can do that broker-locked communities can't**
1. **Completeness and anti-cherry-picking.** If every call is timestamped with the price at posting, scored automatically at a fixed horizon (e.g., one week), and can't be deleted, the record is complete. Robinhood's is opt-in per trade with hideable stats. "Every call counts" is the clearest differentiator against Robinhood.
2. **Cross-broker neutrality.** It works for Schwab, Fidelity, IBKR, Webull, eToro and Robinhood users alike, and for people with no account at all. Broker communities can only verify their own customers.
3. **Forecasts, not positions.** Users can record bearish calls without shorting, calls on stocks they don't own, and calls from people with restricted accounts. That includes licensed professionals, whom Robinhood excludes; the app's own compliance policy would govern them.
4. **Privacy.** Scoring on percentage moves from posting price reveals no dollar P&L, position size or portfolio allocation, all of which Robinhood Social surfaces when users opt in.
5. **Standard, comparable scoring.** One methodology across all users and tickers produces leaderboards that are comparable. Broker P&L profiles mix position sizes, holding periods and selective posting.
6. **No order-flow incentive.** It can credibly claim not to profit from users trading more. Robinhood's Quick trade and live-earnings trading are designed to generate trades.
7. **Optional hybrid.** The app could later add read-only, opt-in brokerage linking to show a "verified holder" badge alongside the call score. This is untested here, and its feasibility, cost and regulatory treatment were not researched.

**Risks to weigh**
- Robinhood could add "lock all trades public" or forecast-style calls quickly.
- X could add prediction or scoring features to cashtags.
- The calls-on-the-record mechanic is easy to copy. The defensible assets are a community, a long scored history, and cross-broker neutrality, not the feature itself.

### Gaps
- There is no data on whether retail users value verified P&L over scored calls, or on adoption and retention of Robinhood Social. No engagement data has been published.
- No evidence was found on how Robinhood Social handles deleted posts or closed-trade history, or whether profit rate counts unposted trades. The beta release says profiles show "past trades" and profit rate, but the GA reporting says stats can be hidden.
- Regulatory constraints on standalone "calls" scoring (e.g., treatment of investment advice or touting) were not researched.
